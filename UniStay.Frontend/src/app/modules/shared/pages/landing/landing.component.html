What is UniStay?

A smarter way to manage student housing

UniStay is a comprehensive dormitory management system designed to streamline every aspect of student housing. From room allocation and bed assignments to maintenance requests and community announcements, it provides a seamless digital experience.

Our mission is to create a comfortable, safe, and efficient living environment so students can focus on their studies and enjoy university life to the fullest.
